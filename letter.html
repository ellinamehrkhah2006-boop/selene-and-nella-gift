TO MY DEAR NELLA

My dearest Nella,

I don't think four years are enough to describe everything our friendship means to me. So many conversations, so many laughs, and so many little moments have become a part of my life.

I hope you know how special you are. Not just because of the big things you do, but because of all the little ways you make ordinary days better.

I made this little place for us because I wanted somewhere to keep a piece of our story. Our memories, our songs, our silly little things, and everything that makes this friendship ours.

I hope the year ahead brings you happiness, beautiful surprises, new adventures, and everything your heart has been wishing for.

And I hope we get to fill many more pages together.

With all my love, YOUR SELENE ♡
